# Fungi Field Key

Offline phone version (installable web app) of a personal field key to British fungi: form, genus and species keys, cited species accounts, look-alike tables, and finds and forays recording.

Open https://ldgough.github.io/fungi-field-key/ in Chrome on the phone, choose "Add to Home screen", and open it once with signal; it then works without signal.

Finds, forays, photographs and names entered in the app are stored only on the phone that recorded them. Nothing is sent to this site.

Photographs are from iNaturalist under the licences credited on the app's Sources and licences screen (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC). Fonts: SIL Open Font Licence. Libraries: exifr (MIT), SheetJS Community Edition (Apache 2.0), JSZip (MIT), licences in `lib/`.

Identification aid only. Never use it to decide whether a fungus is safe to eat.
